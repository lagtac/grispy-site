Grispy — Guide

How to use it, what each message means, and what it cannot do.

New to Grispy? Start with saving and filling a form. Most of what follows assumes you have saved one form at least once, because that is the moment Grispy stops being empty and starts being useful.

The pages

  • Saving and filling a form

    The core of it. Save a form under a name, keep as many sets of answers against it as you need, switch between them, and fill any of them back in. Also: what each screen of the popup means, and how values appear in the save list.

  • What a fill reports

    Grispy checks every field after writing to it, which is why the number it reports is sometimes lower than what you see on the page. What Filled 7 of 10 fields. counts, what "could not be verified" means, and the one thing the message deliberately does not tell you.

  • Protecting your saved data

    The passphrase: what it encrypts, what it deliberately leaves readable, and why a forgotten one cannot be recovered by anyone, us included. Read this before you rely on it.

  • The Settings page

    Everything Grispy has saved, in one list. Deleting any part of it, exporting a backup, restoring one, and the reset that works even when you cannot unlock.

  • Forms that span several pages

    Multi-step wizards. The line that says which step Grispy thinks you are on, the one click that corrects it, and the two kinds of wizard it cannot follow. The longest page here, and the one to read if a form hands you a "Next" button.

  • Forms that repeat a set of fields

    Add-a-participant and add-a-line forms. How rows are saved, the question Grispy asks before it would drop any, and the routine that fills them all.

If something is wrong

Every page here ends with a list of the messages it covers and the limits it knows about, so the fastest way to explain a surprise is usually to find the message you saw. If none of them fit, or a form is handled badly, the support page says what to send — a form Grispy cannot fill is the single most useful thing you can report.
