Grispy — Terms of use

Effective 3 September 2026. Applies to the Grispy browser extension for Chrome and Firefox.

Grispy is free, and stays free. Every form, every saved answer, no limit, no account and nothing to buy.

What free means

Free is the whole product, not a trial of it. It does not expire, it does not count your forms, and it does not ask you to upgrade to finish something you started. If Grispy can fill a form, this version fills it — at home or at work, as often as you like.

What is being built, and what it will cost

Grispy is still being developed. Some of what is coming is meant for people who fill forms as part of their job, in volume, and those capabilities will need a paid licence.

That is a statement about things that do not exist yet. Nothing you can do in Grispy today needs a licence, and nothing you can do today will ever start needing one.

Everything in this version stays free. So do two things still being built: reusing one saved set of details across different forms, and being told when a detail you saved somewhere has gone out of date. Neither of those will sit behind a licence.

There is no price list yet, no date and nothing to buy. If you would like to hear when there is, write to dchrysomallis@outlook.com and say so.

What you may not do with it

Being free to use does not make Grispy free to pass on. You may not redistribute it, resell it, or publish a modified version of it. Grispy is proprietary software and the source is not public.

What Grispy does not collect

Grispy makes no network requests of any kind. There is no account, no login, and no server that knows you exist. The privacy policy sets that out in full, and it is written so you can check it rather than take our word for it.

One consequence is worth stating plainly: because we cannot see your installation, we also cannot recover anything from it. The support page explains what that means in practice.

Changes to these terms

If these terms change, the effective date above changes with them. The promise above — that everything free in this version stays free — is not something a later version of this page can take back.

Questions

Questions about these terms go to dchrysomallis@outlook.com, the same address as everything else.
